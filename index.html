Introduction

        Historically, metal music has always been seen as too aggressive or sometimes satanic in nature (Tsai et al., 2010). 
        However recent studies have shown how the metal community appears to be one of the most welcoming available, 
        acting as a hub for all people of oddities that don’t feel they fit in anywhere else (Herbst, J.-P. (ed.) (2023)). 
        There is also evidence to suggest that listening to aggressive music helps well-being and improves mental health allowing people to exert themselves in a 
        healthy manner (Herbst, J.-P. (ed.) (2023)). This leads to people wanting to get involved musically, 
        with one of the most common methods being singing (Herbst, J.-P. (ed.) (2023)). 
        This is why knowing the different types of singing, or more commonly known as screaming, helps people find their place.
